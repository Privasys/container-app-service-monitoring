About these pages

An app's team turns monitoring on and chooses whether to publish its page. Each page shows the app's uptime over 90 days, its incidents and the updates its team writes on them, and lets you verify any day's figure against the monitor's append-only ledger. Pages that are not published do not appear here.

Watched and served from inside a confidential VM by container-app-service-monitoring, open source under the AGPL-3.0 licence. JSON.
